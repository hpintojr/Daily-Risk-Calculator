The Rules

  1. Your stop comes from the chart. Your size comes from your risk. Never the other way around.
  2. Risk is always calculated on the starting balance, not the current one. Drawdown does not punish you twice.
  3. If you drop back below a tier threshold, the calculator drops your risk automatically. Honor it.
  4. Round position size down. The cap is a cap, not a target.
  5. Three full losses and the session is closed. No revenge trades. The market opens again tomorrow.
  6. Protect the capital first. Consistency comes from that, not the other way around.

How To Use This

  1. Set your Starting Account Balance. This is the size your prop account started at (commonly $50,000 on FTMO, Apex, Topstep, MyFundedFX). It anchors every risk number here and doesn't change for the life of the account.
  2. Set your Current Account Balance before every session. Your tier and per-trade dollar risk follow this number.
  3. Read your stop loss off the chart. Market structure decides your stop, not your account size or how much you want to trade.
  4. Your position size and daily kill switch now calculate themselves. Nothing left to decide under stress.

Why This Works

Most traders blow accounts because they widen the stop to justify a position size, or they double size after a loss trying to win it back. This tool forces the opposite: the stop is fixed by the chart, the size is fixed by the risk, and the daily loss cap is fixed by discipline — not by how the day is going.

This is risk sizing. It is not a trading strategy, an entry signal, or a guarantee of results. Sizing alone doesn't get an account funded — it keeps you in the game long enough for a real edge to compound.
